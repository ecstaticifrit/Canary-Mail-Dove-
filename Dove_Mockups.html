Activation

A1

Defaults undercut the core promise

What I saw

Notify me about and Badge count both default to All emails, and Read receipts are on. Dove's pitch is alerts only for emails that need you.

Why it hurts

Every email interrupts, so the user decides Dove is another client. Read receipts on by default can also expose users to their own recipients.

Fix

After setup, ask "Only notify me about Focus emails?" with Yes preselected. Turn read receipts off by default and make them an opt-in in the composer.
