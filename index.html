Activation

A1

Defaults undercut the core promise

ImpactHighEffortLowMust have

The problem

Notify me about and Badge count both default to All emails, and Read receipts are on. Dove's pitch is alerts only for emails that need you.

Why it matters

Every email interrupts, so the user decides Dove is another client. Read receipts on by default can also expose users to their own recipients.

Fix

After setup, ask "Only notify me about Focus emails?" with Yes preselected. Turn read receipts off by default and make them an opt-in in the composer.

A2

The trial is short, card-gated and inconsistent

ImpactHighEffortMediumMust have

The problem

Seven days with a card upfront and billing from day 8. The sidebar says the Pro trial is running, yet the Upgrade sheet offers "Start your free trial for 7 days, $0.00 due today".

Why it matters

One work cycle is too short for the classifier to learn. A day-8 charge invites "forgot to cancel" refunds, and mixed copy leaves the user unsure whether they are on the trial.

Fix

Make the sheet state-aware, start the clock on activation, send a day-5 summary with one-tap cancel, and test a no-card trial against card-upfront.

A3

The free tier never demonstrates the product's true value

ImpactHighEffortMediumShould have

The problem

Free is 10 AI actions a day, shared across Compose, Wingman, Summaries and Ask. Today, Tasks, Memory, Rules, Smart Labels and Smart Notifications are Pro-only.

Why it matters

Sorting is the core habit and AI actions are a bonus. On day 8 the user loses the views that set Dove apart, hits the cap quickly, and has little reason to come back.

Fix

Keep a paid boundary but move it. Free keeps the inbox, sorting, a basic Today list and Focus-only notifications. Add a usage meter and a downgrade preview.

A4

The Teach Dove feature is semi-functional and basic

ImpactHighEffortHighShould have

The problem

Teach Dove is mostly a sender-to-category rule, reachable only by right-clicking an email that is already misplaced.

Why it matters

The same sender sends mail of very different importance. A rule that is too broad buries mail in Noise, and one that is too narrow leaves Focus cluttered.

Fix

Rules beyond the sender, plain-language rules with a preview, a one-tap "Wrong?" on the row, a readable Rules page and a safety net.

A single-session lock adds friction for anyone who reopens mail in a new tab out of habit, and it hides the product at the moment of return.

Fix

Remove the single-session limit. If one has to stay, show a banner over a working inbox instead of a black screen.

A6

ImpactHighEffortMediumMust have

The problem

A "Needs you" chip said 7:00 PM for a role closing at 1:30 PM. Send check did not fire on a sloppy message. The Calendar was empty although Today mentioned a lecture.

Why it matters

A wrong deadline is the most expensive error Dove can make. Promised features that do nothing, or never explain why, cost trust in week one.

Fix

Show the deadline from the email and say where it came from. Show Send check once as an example, or say it is off. Prompt to connect a calendar and offer events Dove found.

ImpactHighEffortMediumMust have

The problem

Every row already had an AI summary, a label and often a deadline chip before I did anything. I found no control for AI reading per email or sender.

Why it matters

Full-mailbox reading with no notice or control feels like a surprise, and some mail (legal, health, HR) should be left out.

Fix

Say what is read and stored before the first sync, allow exclusions with a visible marker, and add a Privacy page with a toggle per feature and a delete action.

The problem

Fix

Test role-based onboarding that presets Areas and Teach Dove rules, plus a student price or an annual-only discount. Judge it by retained paying users.

Dove detects the deadline and then drops it. The user has to copy the date into the Add event box by hand.

Fix

An event chip on the email and row, an action in the right-click menu, selected-row toolbar and Needs you cards, plus an optional banner and a consent-based auto-add.
